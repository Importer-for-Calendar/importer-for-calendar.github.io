How It Works

1. Prepare Your Spreadsheet

Open any spreadsheet app. Structure columns for dates, times, titles, and details as needed—no rigid templates required.

2. Import and Map

Drag your file into Importer. Map columns to event fields, set durations, recurrences, and alarms intuitively.

3. Review and Convert

Preview events with time zones and rules applied. Process locally for instant .ics export or direct Calendar import.

4. Done

Events appear in Apple Calendar or your chosen app. Repeat as needed.
